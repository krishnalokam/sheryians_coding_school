# What is CP & SP

CP - Cost Price
SP - Selling Price

example - Aman bought the grocery for 2000 INR and sold all of that in local market for 2500 INR
Cost Price (CP) = 2000
Selling price(SP) = 2500

    if SP > CP, profit = SP - CP = 2500 - 2000 = 500 INR

    if CP > SP, loss = CP - SP
