# Progression

The process of moving gradually to a state or position is known as progression.
and when we progress through this numbers we call it Arithmetic Progression.

**series1** : 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12

**series2**: 2, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12

**series1** is an Arithmetic progression as the common diffference is 2 through out the series.

2, 4, 6, 8, 10

Initial term (a) = 2
Common difference (d) = 2
an = nth term, for example, a6 = 6th term = 12

an = a + (n-1)d

    Sum of all numbers = S(n) = n/2 (2a + (n-1)d)

Sum of first 5 terms = S(5) = (5/2)(2*2 + (5-1)*2)
= 5/2(4 + 8)
= 30

    To find total no of elemenets given first term a, and last term l is
     n = (l -a)/d + 1
