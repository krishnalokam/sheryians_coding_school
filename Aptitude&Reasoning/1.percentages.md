# Tricks on percentage

## 1. We need to take percentage of a specific number:

Formula is: (value \* percentage)/100;

ex: 10% of 80 = (80 \* 10 )/100; = 8;

20% of 150 = (150 \* 20 )/ 100 = 30

10% of 156 = 15.6;

### calculate 12% of 500;

    10% of 500 = 50
    1% of 500 = 5;
    1% of 500 = 5

    12% of 500 = 50 + 5 + 5 = 60;

## 2. Trick 2

Example: 20 is what percentage of 80?

    Ans: 25
