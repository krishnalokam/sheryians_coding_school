# Fraction method

Q1) A can do piece of work in 10 days
B can do the same work in 20 days.
if both of them work together, how many days will it take.

/\*
The amount of work A can do in one day - (x/10)
The amount of work B can do in one day - (x/20)

    Together they can do in one day - (x/10) + (x/20)
     = 3x/20 in one day

     1 day  3x/20

     ?      x

     x / (3x/20) = 20/3 = 6.66 days

\*/

Total work = 1unit;
A in 10d = 1unit
1 d = 1/10 unit

b in 1d = 1/20 unit

together work in 1d = 1/10 + 1/20 = 3/20 units;
for I unit = 1/(3/20) = 20/3 = 6.66
