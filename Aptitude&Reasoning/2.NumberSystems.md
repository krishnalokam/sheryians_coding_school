# First we need to know how many types of numbers are there

- Natural Numbers -- All positive numbers from 1 to infinity
- Whole Numbers -- when we include zero in Natural Numbers they became whole numbers.
- Integers -- All counting numbers from 0 to infinity and -1 to negative infinity
- Even and Odd -- divisible by 2 are even , otherwise odd
- Rational Number -- numbers which can be expressed in the form of p/q and have finite decimal structure
- Irrational Number -- infinite decimal structure
- Prime Numbers - Number which is diviisble by 1 and itself are called prime numbers
- Composite Number -- numbers which has more than 2 factors are Composite Numbers
- Factors -- If Number1 is dividing the Number2, that means, Number1 is the factor of Number2
- Co-Prime. -- Co prime are pairs of number which has only one common factor 1.
  eg: (6 and 7 are Co-Prime numbers)

  # Divisibility rule

  ## Divisibility rule of 2: last digit must be, (2,4,6,8,0)

  ## Divisibility rule of 3: Number is divisible by 3 if sum of its digits are divisible by 3

  ## Divisibility rule of 4: Number is divisible by 4 if last two digits of the number are divisible by 4

  ## Divisibility rule of 5: last digit must be either 5 or 0

  ## Divisibility rule of 6: If the number is divisible by both 2 and 3, then it is divisible by 6.

## Divisibility rule of 8:

                If the last 3 digits of the number is divisible by 8 , then the number is divisible by 8.

## Divisibility rule of 9:

                If sum of the digits is divisible by 9, then number is divisible by 9

## Divisibility rule of 11:
