In syllogism, you will have some statements and then you have to tell the right conclusion
according to those statements.

ex. Some ships are boats.All boarts are submarinies.Some submarines are yatches.

Conclusion:

1. Some yatches are boats.
2. Some submarines are boats.
3. Some submarines are ships.
4. Some yatches are ships.

For solving such questions, we follow venn diagrams.

We have three types of statements.
Some
All
None
